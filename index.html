WHO WE ARE

COMMITED TO

IRAQ'S FUTURE

With over two decades of distinguished expertise in construction and EPC services, we have proudly executed transformative projects across Iraq. Firmly rooted in the sectors of Energy and Power, Oil and Gas, Petrochemicals, Bitumen and Base Oil, Construction Materials Manufacturing, Cement Plants, and pioneering innovations in Smart Industry solutions such as  Smart Metering, we stand at the forefront of industrial progress. Our dedicated workforce of 1,200+ professionals drives both project development and Strategic Project Consultancy, bolstered by our international offices in Iraq, the UAE, and the UK, positioning us as a key player in the region’s growth and development.

Milestone

GROUP ESTABLISHED With a focus on Construction and Support Services

The company was initially established to be a player within the newly opened Private Sector in Iraq, delivering construction and support services to Governmental and Corporate entities. Many International companies entering Iraq relied on Harlows support services to establish their local office operations.

Milestone

BRITISH EMBASSY IN BAGHDAD, Expansion and Renovation.

Construction of a new visa facility as well as the expansion and restoration of diplomatic housing.

Milestone

Al Rasheed hotel renovation project prior to arab summit in 2012

Comprehensive refurbishment of the only famous (Al Rasheed Hotel) in Baghdad's Green Zone was completed before the Arab Summit in 2012.

Milestone

Prime minister's office the ziggurat palace

A thorough overhaul and restoration of the iraqi prime minister's office in Baghdad.

Milestone

Bismayah new City Project

The largest housing project in Iraq's recent history, Harlow performed economic research and feasibility assessments, and provided Hanwha E&C with administrative, logistical, and legal support in addition to building the project's main labor complex

Milestone

Al Suwayarah Air Base

Formerly Shaykh Mazhar Air Base, building the temporary resident camps for 324 security soldiers and main base camp for long-term construction work for Korea Aerospace Industries KAI.

Milestone

EXXONMOBIL West Qurna 1 Security

In 2018, Harlow Groups Security Company Al Murabit Security was selected by ExxonMobil as the exclusive security provider for their West Qurna 1 Oilfield project. Our team hired and managed 360 local Iraqi personnel, composed of family members of Martyrs from local tribes.

Milestone

Al-Khairat THERMAL POWER PLANT

Our mega power project in Iraq, 2800 MW HFO fired Thermal Power Plant located 20km south of Karbala.

Milestone

Smart Meters Collection and Electrical Services ltd

Subsidiary of Harlow Group specialized in Smart Metering infrastructure research and development.
