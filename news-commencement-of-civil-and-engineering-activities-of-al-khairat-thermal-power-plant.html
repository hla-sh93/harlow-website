Commencement of Civil and Engineering Activities of Al Khairat Thermal Power Plant

Commencement of Civil and Engineering Activities of Al Khairat Thermal Power Plant

January 22, 2025

The Al Khairat Thermal Power Plant project has officially entered a new phase with the commencement of civil and engineering activities at the site. This milestone marks the start of foundational work, including site preparation, earthmoving, and initial engineering operations critical to the project’s progression.

The project team gathered for a kickoff event at the site to commemorate this significant step, highlighting the collective commitment to achieving project goals. This phase lays the groundwork for constructing and successfully implementing the thermal power plant, reinforcing the team’s dedication to delivering a world-class energy project.
