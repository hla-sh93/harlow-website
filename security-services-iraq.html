AL MURABIT SECURITY SERVICES

YOUR TRUSTED SECURITY PARTNER IN IRAQ

Al-Murabit Security Services working under the Harlow Group is a private security provider that offers a broad portfolio of services to clients in the oil and gas, government, and commercial sectors.

Al-Murabit Security Services provides a range of security services, including armed security, close protection, surveillance, and security consulting. The company also offers a range of other services, such as training, intelligence, and investigations. We have a team of highly experienced and qualified security professionals who are dedicated to providing the highest level of security for their clients.

Established in 2004, Al Murabit Security Services is a premier private security company that specialises in providing high-standard private security, personal security, information security, mobile security, asset protection and Risk Management services in Iraq.

Al–Murabit is one of the leading security consulting companies that design and provide security solutions to mitigate the inherent risks associated with operating in high-risk, complex environments and deliver realistic solutions to enable clients’ business.

About Al Murabit Security Services

Al Murabit’s multinational client base includes organisations operating within the oil and gas, private, non-government and government sectors. Our clients value the support that AMS affords, in particular, our flexible approach to managing security and risk management support functions that enable our client’s business activities across Iraq. When you require industry-leading private security services 24/7, our professionally trained team of security consultants and experts will never disappoint you.
