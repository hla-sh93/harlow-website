Every year, Harlow International extends many of employment offers, and we eagerly welcome qualified people to join our flourishing group.

Here is some important information regarding our procedures that you should be aware of in order to verify the validity of your employment offer.

1. We never request payment from or transfer money to applicants.

2. All correspondence with us will always be direct and originate from an official HR@Harlowinternational.com email address.

When you receive a job offer:

Your employment offer is probably false if it looks too good to be true. If you have reason to believe a job offer is not genuine, keep these three points in mind:

  • 1- Avoid getting in touch with the sender of the message.
  • 2- Avoid sharing any personal information.
  • 3- Do not send any money.

Email us at HR@Harlowinternational.com if you have any questions or want to verify that a job offer is legitimate. Please include a picture or screenshot of the message that you have received.
